# Intraday market-data research

**Research date:** 6 November 2026. The provider assessment below records the original research checkpoint and is not a guarantee of future availability. CoinGecko Demo uses automatic candle intervals of 30 minutes, four hours, or four days, depending on lookback; it does not supply the requested minute intervals.

## Subsequent local prototype

The local source now includes a separate keyless `GET /api/market/intraday/{coinId}?interval=3m|5m|15m&days=1|7` adapter using Binance's fixed public market-data-only host. It supports BTC, ETH, SOL, LINK, LTC and BCH against **USDT**, with no PYUSD pair. USDT is never relabelled as USD or converted into the portfolio's display currency. A full day is supported at each interval; seven days requires 15-minute candles. Missing or zero-trade source slots make the requested window unavailable rather than creating prices or silently shortening it.

Website and native interval controls preserve the independent CoinGecko view. The server uses bounded bodies, deadlines, shared in-process request limits, caching and provider cooldowns. Its HTTPS response Date is labelled as a response date, not an independently verified exchange clock. The code was written independently of the GPL-licensed Relentless trading stack; no trading code, account connection or trading credential was imported. Offline adapter/route and client tests have passed. Current live-route, visual, typecheck and device evidence belongs in the [acceptance record](LOCAL-UPGRADE-ACCEPTANCE-20261106.md). Public endpoint access does not establish commercial redisplay rights; confirm applicable terms and regional access before a hosted product rollout. The [OpenAPI contract](../postman/specs/binance-intraday.yaml) records the exact supported inputs and outputs.

## What was verified

At approximately 12:40–12:50 UTC, bounded, unauthenticated public metadata and sample-candle reads were made from the research host. No account, trading, or provider credentials were used. The table records *pair metadata*, not a promise of liquidity, a customer's right to trade, or permission to redistribute data. Pair listings and status can change.

| Tracked asset | Kraken spot pair list | Coinbase Exchange product list | Binance.com ticker and selected pair metadata |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BTC, ETH, SOL | USD and GBP online | USD and GBP online | USD online; GBP pairs on break |
| LINK, LTC | USD and GBP online | USD and GBP online | USDT online; GBP pairs on break; USD not found in the checked ticker list |
| BCH | USD and GBP online **at check time** | USD and GBP online | USDT online; USD and GBP not found in the checked ticker list |
| PYUSD | USD online; GBP not found | USD delisted; GBP not found | USD, GBP and USDT not found in the checked ticker list |

MEXC's full public spot metadata listed BTC, ETH, SOL, LINK, LTC and BCH against **USDT**, with no direct USD/GBP pairs for the seven assets and no PYUSD pair in that response. USDT is a stablecoin quote, not fiat USD. Binance explicitly identifies its separately listed BTC/USD market as fiat USD; that does not make BTC/USDT a USD market. [Binance USD listing](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/6f4c5397413f47bb832a79ee54423eb7)

**Time-sensitive BCH/GBP change:** Kraken announced that this native market moves to cancel-only on **6 November at 14:00 UTC**, with delisting between 6 and 9 November and a synthetic replacement scheduled by the end of 9 November. Kraken describes synthetic-pair market data, including candles, as approximate and derived from underlying order books. Do not treat the replacement as a native BCH/GBP trade series or assume the old pair remains available. [Migration notice](https://support.kraken.com/gb/articles/native-pairs-moving-to-sythetic-pairs), [synthetic pairs](https://support.kraken.com/articles/synthetic-pairs)

The Kraken sample also exposed sparse trading. In roughly six hours, 119 complete three-minute groups contained at least one trade in 118 BTC/GBP groups, 25 PYUSD/USD groups, 21 LINK/GBP groups and two BCH/GBP groups. Flat zero-trade minutes were present. A small Binance native 3m sample contained trades in only three of nine closed BTC/USD rows and two of nine SOL/USD rows. These are momentary observations, not stable liquidity measures. The command output was transient; no raw response or reproducible local evidence file was retained.

## Provider capabilities and limits

- **Kraken:** [Spot OHLC](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/market-data/get-ohlc-data) supports 1m, 5m and 15m, but not native 3m. The latest item is uncommitted; the endpoint returns at most 720 recent entries, so 1m data covers less than 12 hours of closed history. `since` cannot recover older OHLC rows. Its response has an `error` array that must be checked even on HTTP 200. Bitcoin's REST aliases observed in this assessment were `XBTUSD` and `XBTGBP`, not the display names `XBT/USD` and `XBT/GBP`. Kraken recommends public API calls at one per second or less and rate-limits OHLC by IP and pair. [Rate limits](https://support.kraken.com/gb/articles/206548367-what-are-the-api-rate-limits-)
- **Coinbase Exchange:** [Product candles](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-product-candles) supports 1m, 5m and 15m, but not native 3m. Bucket timestamps are starts in seconds. The documented request maximum is 300 candles; pagination requires bounded time windows, and results can precede the requested start. The default live sample returned 350 closed rows despite that documented maximum, so validate and bound responses rather than assuming a provider response length. Missing intervals may reflect no ticks, but Coinbase also warns historical data may be incomplete. Public requests are IP-limited and return HTTP 429 when limited. [Rate limits](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/exchange/rest-api/rate-limits)
- **Binance.com:** [Spot klines](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-spot-trading/api/rest-api/market) natively support 3m, 5m and 15m, up to 1,000 rows per request. Times are in milliseconds; the documented close time is the inclusive final millisecond. A public 3m BTC/USD and SOL/USD read succeeded from the research host, but most closed rows in the tiny samples had zero trades. Back off on HTTP 429 and respect `Retry-After`; repeated violations can produce HTTP 418. Binance.com and Binance.US are different services. [REST error and rate-limit rules](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/spot/rest-api)
- **MEXC:** [Spot klines](https://mexcdevelop.github.io/apidocs/spot_v3_en/) document 1m, 5m and 15m, but not 3m, with a maximum of 1,000 rows. A BTC/USDT 5m read succeeded from the research host. This is a USDT quote and does not fill the missing fiat or PYUSD coverage.

## Requirements for a later implementation

1. Use a confirmed native spot pair and preserve the exact exchange, base asset, quote currency, interval, retrieval time and actual covered range. Do not substitute a USDT/USDC market for USD, convert USD into GBP without a separately identified and time-aligned FX source, or present exchange prices as PayPal/Venmo execution quotes. Unsupported display currencies or pairs must be visibly unavailable.
2. Discard an uncommitted candle unless the product explicitly labels it as partial. Normalize provider bucket times to one documented UTC convention; for example, an inclusive Binance millisecond close is not the same timestamp as the next bucket's exclusive start. Validate ordering, uniqueness, bounds and OHLC values.
3. Derive 3m only where the provider's 1m data and permissions support it: group three correctly aligned, complete minute intervals of the **same native pair**. For sources that explicitly identify zero-trade minutes, exclude those minutes' carried-forward OHLC values from the calculation; use the first and last trade-bearing opens/closes and extrema across trade-bearing minutes. Emit a gap if all three contain no trades, any required source minute is missing, or completeness cannot be established. Clearly label the result as aggregated from venue 1m candles, not as a native exchange 3m candle.
4. Leave zero-trade direct intervals and missing/invalid data as gaps; do not manufacture prices or bridge them visually. Explain sparse markets and ensure indicator labels do not imply 20 continuous time periods when the calculation uses 20 observed candles. Treat stale, restricted, malformed, rate-limited and unavailable provider responses as explicit unavailable/error states; no silent provider or quote-currency substitution.
5. Respect the provider's actual history window. A Kraken 1m request cannot substantiate a full 24-hour 3m view; either obtain appropriately licensed longer history, offer a shorter clearly labelled span, or mark the selection unavailable. Do not silently present a partial span as the requested lookback.

## Access and rights gates

Kraken is the strongest of these candidates for the observed fiat pair coverage, subject to native-market status, sparse trading and history limitations. Its [API notice](https://docs-legacy.kraken.com/api/docs/guides/global-intro/) requires prior permission for non-personal commercial use of public endpoint data. Coinbase's [market-data terms](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/market_data) restrict external display and derived charts without prior written consent. Confirm the applicable agreement and customer-display rights for any chosen feed before using it in the product; unauthenticated endpoint access is not a redistribution licence. Verify regional access and pair eligibility separately from rights to display public data. For example, Kraken lists PYUSD as unavailable for deposit or trading to EEA clients; that does not itself establish whether public price display is restricted. [Regional restrictions](https://support.kraken.com/articles/where-is-kraken-licensed-or-regulated)

Paxos is a PayPal-aligned lead, not a verified answer. Its [historical-candles documentation](https://docs.paxos.com/api-reference/endpoints/pricing/list-historical-candles) lists PYUSD/GBP in the request enum and 1m, 5m and 15m increments, but requires an OAuth token with `exchange:historical_prices`. The response market enum does not list GBP/PYUSD and the documentation says unavailable markets return 400. Actual access, pair status, native-trade provenance, available history and display rights remain unverified. No Paxos credential or endpoint was used.
