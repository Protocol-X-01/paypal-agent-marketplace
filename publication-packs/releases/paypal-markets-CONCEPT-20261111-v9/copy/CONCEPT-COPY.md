# PayPal Markets — INDEPENDENT CONCEPT

A hackathon design study that places clearer public crypto-market references and personal estimates alongside the wallet customers already use, with Channel3 product discovery and a separate fictional Operations preview. No official affiliation or endorsement is claimed.

## Short description

PayPal Markets explores a calmer way to follow public crypto-market movements, understand price history, enter personal portfolio estimates and discover products. The concept keeps Venmo wallet activity separate and does not connect to a customer wallet, move funds, trade crypto or complete a purchase.

## Demonstrated in the included captures

- Historical local website and market views using public CoinGecko reference data.
- Historical local AI commentary on public BTC/GBP candle data. It may be inaccurate and is not investment advice.
- Expo Web phone and tablet responsive views for personal tools, Shop discovery controls and empty Business report inputs.
- A separate disconnected Operations concept using invented aggregate figures.
