# PayPal Markets — concept identity quick start

This is an independent hackathon design study. PayPal and Venmo names and marks belong to their owners. This pack does not imply affiliation, approval, endorsement or a trademark licence. Keep **INDEPENDENT CONCEPT** visible anywhere the promotional artwork is shown.

## Visual direction

- Deep navy `#001C64` and PayPal blue `#0070E0` anchor the layout.
- Vivid sky blue `#00CCFF` carries the market line and focus accents.
- Pale `#F5F7FB` surfaces keep the charts and cards legible.
- The palette is a project concept, not official PayPal brand guidance.
- Keep the supplied PayPal identity intact. Do not redraw it, change its proportions or use concept artwork as a replacement logo.

## Choosing assets

- Start with `artwork/concept-cover-wide.png` for an internal presentation cover and `artwork/concept-social-square.png` for a square concept preview. Both already show an independent-concept label.
- Use `captures/account-phone.png`, `captures/shop-phone.png` and `captures/business-tablet-fictional.png` only with the captions and surface descriptions in `ASSET-INDEX.json`. These are Expo Web captures, not Android or iOS device proof.
- `captures/web-home-sky-public.png` and `captures/ai-market-public.png` are historical local web captures from 6 November 2026. Their public market values are historical reference data, not execution prices.
- The Operations captures use an explicitly fictional disconnected fixture. Do not present them as PayPal internal monitoring or real customer data.
