## Payment and agent-wallet boundary

This concept pack does not demonstrate customer wallet access, an agent-controlled spendable wallet, PYUSD transfer, live merchant checkout, subscription management or a completed purchase. A testnet walkthrough may demonstrate the breadth of the agent experience, but test tokens have no monetary value and do not establish that live payments work. Channel3 listed its Checkout API as coming soon when reviewed on 11 November 2026.
