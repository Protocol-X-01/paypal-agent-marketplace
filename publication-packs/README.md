## Read the evidence carefully

- Market and AI screenshots show historical public-market context. They are not current execution prices, personalized advice or connected account data.
- Expo Web images are browser previews from 6 November 2026, not Android or iOS device acceptance.
- Operations metrics are invented examples. They do not show PayPal's internal customer activity.
- The pack records screenshots from a local working tree, including uncommitted source, but does not contain the application source or prove the current build or hosted state.
- Live sign-in, wallet balances, payments, crypto trading, real Business reporting, internal monitoring, current native acceptance and production readiness are not established.
